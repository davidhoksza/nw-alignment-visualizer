Affine Gap Alignment Stepper

Global alignment with affine gaps

Needleman–Wunsch with three matrices. G holds the best score for each pair of prefixes, H the best that ends with a horizontal move (a letter of S1 faces a gap) and V the best that ends with a vertical move (a letter of S2 faces a gap). Step through the fill and the traceback and watch where every value comes from.

Gap of length k costsopen + (k−1)·extend
G↘best overall: H, V, or the diagonal plus the letter score
H→horizontal move, reads the cell to the left: S1[j] faces a gap
V↓vertical move, reads the cell above: S2[i] faces a gap

In every cell H and V are computed first, then G. Click any cell to jump to the step that computed it. The small letter in a cell's corner names the matrix its value came from. Keys: ← → step, Space play or pause, Home and End jump.
